WiggMap — Where is the grass actually greener? Compare 160+ countries

WiggMap is the data-driven tool that helps expats, digital nomads, retirees and global citizens decide where to live, work or retire abroad. We compare more than 160 countries on 25+ data points: net average and minimum salaries, studio rent, electricity, water and mobile costs, beer and coffee prices, gasoline, VAT and income tax brackets, immigration ease, public healthcare quality, sun hours per year, crime index, purchasing power and religion snapshot.
