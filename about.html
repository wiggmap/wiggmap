About the platform

Not a travel blog. A decision tool.

WiggMap was built around a single question: where is life actually better for someone like me? Not the tourist version. Not the influencer version. The real one — with real salaries, real rents, real taxes, and real tradeoffs.

The web is full of "best countries to live in" lists based on vibes. WiggMap is built differently. Every country page shows indicative figures for income, cost of living, tax pressure, healthcare quality, safety index, climate, immigration ease, and more — side by side, in a format you can actually use.

It's built by one person, continuously improved, and trilingual by design — because the expat question is asked in French, Spanish, and English just as much as in one language.

Who it's for

Built for people making real decisions.

WiggMap is for the person who's wondering whether to move to Portugal or Thailand. The remote worker choosing between Tbilisi and Mexico City. The family comparing healthcare and schooling across borders. The retiree calculating whether their pension stretches further in Southeast Asia or Eastern Europe.

It's also for the curious — people who just want to understand how the world actually works, beyond the fantasy version sold in glossy magazines. Real salaries. Real costs. Real tradeoffs. No filter.

Transparency

An honest platform, still growing.

🚧
WiggMap is actively developed. Figures are indicative and updated regularly. Some country profiles are more complete than others. We always signal when data is approximate — and we never pretend otherwise.

If you spot outdated or incorrect data, use the "Correct data" button on any country page. Every correction is reviewed and genuinely appreciated — the platform gets better because of it.

WiggMap is a solo project. No VC funding, no growth-hacking playbook. Just an obsessive attempt to build something genuinely useful for people navigating one of life's biggest decisions.
