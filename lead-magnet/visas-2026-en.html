Retirement Visas

10 official residence programs to retire abroad in 2026 — from low-cost Asia to premium Europe.

1
🇵🇹 Portugal D7 Visa Portugal
~€870/month income Renewable, 5-year path Difficulty: ★★★★☆

The European benchmark. You need to prove passive income (pension, rental, dividends) at least matching the Portuguese minimum wage. Leads to permanent residency after 5 years and citizenship after 5 years too. Local health coverage required.

Best for: European retirees seeking mild climate and reasonable taxes
2
🇵🇦 Panama Pensionado Panama
$1,000/month pension Permanent immediately Difficulty: ★★★★★

The simplest retirement visa on the planet. A guaranteed lifetime pension of $1,000+/month is enough. In return: immediate permanent residency, territorial taxation (foreign income untaxed), and a famous discount card (50% on entertainment, 25% on restaurants, 30% on transport). You can apply from your home country.

Best for: modest pensioners, crypto holders, foreign-income earners
3
🇲🇾 Malaysia MM2H Malaysia
~$10,000/month income 5–20 years Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

"Malaysia My Second Home" — tightened in 2024. Three tiers (Silver/Gold/Platinum) based on a frozen bank deposit (150K–1M MYR). Grants 5 to 20 years of residency. No local work allowed, but foreign passive income is exempted. World-class private healthcare at a fraction of European prices.

Best for: well-off retirees who want Asia without the language barrier
4
🇹🇭 Thailand LTR Visa Thailand
~$80,000/year income 10 years renewable Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

The Long-Term Resident visa launched in 2022 targets affluent profiles. Four tracks: Wealthy Global Citizens, Wealthy Pensioners, Work-from-Thailand Pros, Highly-Skilled. 10-year renewable residency, multi-entry, special 17% tax rate, work permit included for the Pros track, dependents included (spouse + 4 children).

Best for: well-off retirees or high-income remote workers
5
🇪🇸 Spain Non-Lucrative Visa Spain
~€28,800/year 1 year, then 2+2+5 Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

For non-active applicants with sufficient passive income. No work rights. Replaces the now-defunct Golden Visa (scrapped in late 2024). Lets you become a Spanish tax resident — useful for European tax rates and access to Spain's healthcare system.

Best for: European retirees seeking warm climate and proximity
6
🇨🇷 Costa Rica Pensionado Costa Rica
$1,000/month pension 2 years renewable Difficulty: ★★★★☆

One of the easiest residences in the Americas. Lifetime pension of $1,000+/month is the only requirement. The country: no army, stable democracy, jaw-dropping biodiversity, life expectancy 80 years (the Nicoya Peninsula is one of the world's "blue zones" of longevity). English widely spoken.

Best for: outdoor retirees, nature & wellness profiles
7
🇲🇽 Mexico Residente Temporal Mexico
~$2,600/month 1 year then 4 years Difficulty: ★★★★★

Temporary residency renewable up to 4 years, then convertible to Residente Permanente. Show stable monthly income over the past 6 months, OR savings ($43,000+). Thriving expat communities in Mérida, San Miguel de Allende, Puerto Vallarta. Excellent and affordable private healthcare.

Best for: American, Canadian and European retirees
8
🇪🇨 Ecuador Pensionado Visa Ecuador
$1,410/month pension 2 years → permanent Difficulty: ★★★★★

One of the lowest entry barriers in the world. 3× the Ecuadorian minimum wage (~$1,410/month) is enough. Dollarized since 2000 — no currency risk. Cuenca attracts thousands of American retirees for its eternal-spring climate and low-cost healthcare.

Best for: retirees who want Latin America without complex paperwork
9
🇵🇭 Philippines SRRV Philippines
$10K–20K deposit For life Difficulty: ★★★★☆

"Special Resident Retiree's Visa" — lifetime residency in exchange for a frozen bank deposit ($10K for 50+ with pension, $20K without pension). No monthly income threshold. English is an official language. One of the only Asian nations giving you immediate permanent residency this easily.

Best for: English-speaking retirees, beach & island lovers
10
🇬🇷 Greece FIP Visa Greece
~€3,500/month 2 years + renewals Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

"Financially Independent Person" — for retirees with solid passive income (pension, dividends, rental). Unlocks Greece's flat 7% tax regime on foreign income for 15 years (one of the most attractive in Europe). Affordable real estate outside Athens and Mykonos.

Best for: retirees seeking soft taxes and the Mediterranean

Digital Nomad Visas

10 visas built for remote workers — from European Portugal to Bali via tax-free Dubai.

11
🇵🇹 Portugal D8 Visa Portugal
~€3,480/month 1 year + 2-year renewal Difficulty: ★★★★☆

The D8 launched in late 2022. You need 4× the Portuguese minimum wage in active income (employment contract or freelance). Lets you become a tax resident and access the NHR regime (10% flat tax on foreign income for 10 years, under conditions). A long-term European foothold.

Best for: European freelancers seeking a permanent base
12
🇪🇸 Spain Digital Nomad Visa Spain
~€2,760/month 3 years + 2 years Difficulty: ★★★★☆

Launched in 2023 as part of the "Startup Law". Threshold: 2× the Spanish minimum wage (~€2,760/month). The Beckham regime is accessible: 24% flat tax on income up to €600K for 6 years (vs. up to 47% normally). Family included. Apply via the Spanish consulate.

Best for: developers and consultants earning €60–150K/year
13
🇮🇩 Indonesia E33G KITAS (Bali) Indonesia
$60,000/year income 1 year renewable Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

The KITAS Remote Worker E33G is officially Indonesia's nomad visa. Requires $60,000 annual income over the past 12 months + a contract with a foreign company. Renewable. Bali welcomed 5+ million visitors in 2024, a sizeable share being long-stay nomads.

Best for: high-earning developers, Asia-based creators
14
🇲🇾 Malaysia DE Rantau Malaysia
$24,000/year 12 months + renewal Difficulty: ★★★★☆

"Digital Nomad pass" launched in late 2022. Very low threshold ($24K annual income) accessible to many freelancers. IT tier expanded in 2024. Multi-entry included. KL is a perfect base to explore Southeast Asia with fast internet and contained costs.

Best for: tech freelancers earning $30–60K/year, Asia hub seekers
15
🇨🇴 Colombia Digital Nomad Visa Colombia
~$680/month 2 years Difficulty: ★★★★★

One of the world's lowest entry barriers: 3× the Colombian minimum wage (~$680/month). Renewable up to 2 years total. Medellín is the Latin nomad capital: eternal spring climate, huge expat scene, very affordable cost of living by Western standards.

Best for: junior nomads, freelancers starting their careers
16
🇧🇷 Brazil VITEM XIV Brazil
$1,500/month or $18K bank 1 year + 1-year renewal Difficulty: ★★★★☆

"Immigrant Worker — Remote Worker" visa created in 2022. Very accessible. Florianópolis and Rio attract nomads for their lifestyle. Rare perk: you can import personal goods tax-free. Family inclusion possible.

Best for: Spanish/Portuguese-speaking nomads, beach-lifestyle profiles
17
🇦🇪 Dubai Virtual Working Visa United Arab Emirates
$5,000/month 1 year renewable Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

Launched in 2021 by Dubai. Requires $5K monthly income + health insurance + a foreign-employer contract. 0% personal income tax. Excellent internet, top safety, but high cost of living (rent is roughly 3× Bangkok). Massive expat community (90% of the population).

Best for: tech/finance profiles earning $80K+/year, tax-optimization minded
18
🇬🇪 Remotely from Georgia Georgia
$2,000/month 1 year Difficulty: ★★★★★

Official program + the "Individual Entrepreneur" status with 1% tax up to $155K/year in revenue. Tbilisi and Batumi host a fast-growing nomad scene. Wine, mountains, legendary hospitality. Visa-free entry for 1 year for most Europeans and Americans.

Best for: freelancers seeking ultra-favorable tax status
19
🇨🇷 Costa Rica Estancia Costa Rica
$3,000/month 1 year + 1-year renewal Difficulty: ★★★★☆

Launched in 2022. Requires $3K/month stable income (or $4K with family). Perks: foreign income exempt from local tax, work equipment imports tax-free, ability to open a local bank account. Pura vida, surfing, biodiversity.

Best for: experienced nomads seeking nature and stability
20
🇪🇪 Estonia Digital Nomad Visa Estonia
€3,504/month 1 year Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

The world's first official nomad visa (August 2020). Pair it with e-Residency: you can register an Estonian company entirely online (only 20% tax on dividends). Tallinn is one of Europe's most digitally connected capitals. Schengen access included.

Best for: tech entrepreneurs, nomads needing an EU base

Skilled Work Visas

5 major destinations to settle long-term and work for a local employer.

21
🇩🇪 Germany EU Blue Card Germany
~€45,300/year 4 years + permanent residency Difficulty: ★★★★☆

The German version of the European Blue Card, overhauled in 2023 to become one of Europe's easiest. The threshold is lowered for shortage occupations (~€41K/year). Lets you move across the EU after 12 months. Family is immediately eligible. Permanent residency after 27 months (21 with B1-level German).

Best for: engineers, doctors, English-speaking tech profiles
22
🇨🇦 Canada Express Entry Canada
CRS score (variable) Permanent residency direct Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

Federal points-based system. Three sub-programs: Federal Skilled Worker, Canadian Experience Class, Federal Skilled Trades. Grants permanent residency directly (not a temporary work visa). CRS score depends on age, languages, experience, education. The royal road into Canada.

Best for: 25–40-year-olds with degrees, French speakers (bonus points)
23
🇦🇺 Australia Skills in Demand Australia
~AUD 73,150/year minimum 2–4 years + residency Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

A new visa launched in late 2024, replacing the 482 TSS. Three streams: Specialist Skills (AUD 135K+), Core Skills (AUD 73K+), Essential Skills (under construction). Employer sponsorship required. Leads to permanent residency in 2–4 years depending on the stream.

Best for: tech, healthcare, listed shortage occupations
24
🇸🇬 Singapore Employment Pass Singapore
~SGD 5,600/month minimum 2 years + renewable Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

The EP moved to a COMPASS scoring system in 2023. Minimum ~SGD 5,600/month for most jobs (up to ~SGD 11,800 in finance). You can bring your spouse and children via Dependant's Pass. Global financial hub with moderate taxes.

Best for: finance, senior tech, international profiles
25
🇳🇱 Netherlands Highly Skilled Migrant Netherlands
~€5,700/month (30+) 5 years Difficulty: ★★★★☆

Paired with the famous "30% ruling": 30% of your salary tax-exempt for 5 years (under conditions). Fast application process via a recognised employer (sponsor). Amsterdam and Eindhoven have become major European tech hubs. English is everywhere.

Best for: senior tech, international profiles with 5+ years of experience
