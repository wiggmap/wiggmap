What the numbers tell you

Average salaries tell a stark story: Singapore workers earn $5,500/month on average vs $1,170/month in Malaysia — a 4.7× gap that reflects fundamentally different economic tiers. Housing costs follow the same direction: a studio in regional Singapore averages $2,200/month vs $520/month in Malaysia — Malaysia is the cheaper base for renters.

Safety is a meaningful differentiator here. Singapore scores 20 / 100 on the crime index vs 45 / 100 for Malaysia — a gap that shapes daily life, insurance costs and the mental load of living abroad.

Singapore is for high-income professionals seeking Asia's most efficient city-state: zero crime, world-class infrastructure and no capital gains tax — at the cost of high living expenses. Malaysia is for expats seeking an English-speaking, multicultural Asian base with strong infrastructure, affordable living and the popular MM2H long-stay visa.
